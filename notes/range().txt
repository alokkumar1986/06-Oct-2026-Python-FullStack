range() :  It is method/function. It is used to generate sequence of numbers.

sequence type : str, list, tuple and range()

example : 

print(range(10))

3 types of form :

1) range(10)  : range(0, 10)

2) range(2, 10) : range(2, 10)

3) range(2, 10, 2) : range(2, 10, 2)
